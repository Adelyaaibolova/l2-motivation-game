# L2 Motivation Challenge

An interactive game for testing your knowledge of L2 (second language) motivation theory.

**▶ Play here:** https://adelyaaibolova.github.io/l2-motivation-game/

## What's inside

1. **Match the model**: match L2 motivation models with their descriptions and components.
2. **Sort by era**: sort concepts into the periods of L2 motivation research.
3. **Memory: who said it?**: pair each researcher with their model or concept.
4. **★ Bonus: vision training**: put the six phases of the visionary training programme in order.

The content is based on Dörnyei, Z., & Ryan, S. (2015). *The Psychology of the Language Learner Revisited*, Chapter 4: Motivation.

## About

This game was made during a seminar in my semester in Hungary, at Károli Gáspár University of the Reformed Church in Hungary (Budapest), as a mini project for the course *Individual differences in language learning*. It was fun to create an interactive task for my classmates!
